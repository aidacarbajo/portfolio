Scope: the portfolio home page (EN `/`, ES `/es/`). Mode: Persuade.
Audience: startups and product teams, founders with a new app, small businesses needing a website, recruiters. Action: email Aida.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Nothing launches until every item reads CHECKED, and Aida runs that checklist for your product. Refuses the standard portfolio: name left and photo right, a row of service cards, a big-number stats strip.

OWN-WORLD: Cockpit navy panels (#0b2a4a) own the opening and the close. Laminated checklist cards in cool white with condensed caps and dot leaders in challenge/response form. Amber annunciator lights, green CHECKED responses. Air-traffic flight strips in holders hold the services. A logbook page holds the proof. A cabin window frames the portrait. Barlow Condensed for display, Barlow for text, both self-hosted. No gradients or glass.

STORY: In one glance the visitor sees who she is (name, QA Engineer and web developer) and what she will check for them. Proof comes from the logbook and PackValet. The page's own pre-flight check runs live. The visitor clears the launch by emailing her.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Navy field. "AIDA CARBAJO" in huge condensed caps top-left, with her role under it. The portrait sits in a cabin window on the right, and its shade lifts on load. Over the lower left, a laminated "Pre-launch checklist" card lists four services as challenge/response rows that tick to CHECKED one after another. Its last row is "Cleared for launch" with the primary button "Let's talk".

FORM: my own grounded candidate ranked 1 (pick card), aviation pre-flight checklist. seed: none (unavailable). Signature interaction: the window shade lifts, then the checklist ticks, all on one ease-out clock. Motion grammar: strips slide into their holders, logbook numbers roll like an odometer, controls press to 0.97.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

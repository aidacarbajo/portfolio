## Users

- Startups and product teams that need tests before shipping to production.
- Founders with a new web or mobile app who want it tested before launch.
- Small businesses and services that need a website built from scratch.

All four are confirmed audiences; no single one is primary.

Constraints:
- Her paid work experience is QA. adidas is mentioned only as her current job, never as the frame for everything.
